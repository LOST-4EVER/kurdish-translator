Studio Workstation Guide & Shortcuts

Fast keyboard shortcuts and gesture workflow

Playback Controls

  • Space: Play / Pause
  • ← / →: Step back/forward 1 cue (or 1s)
  • Shift + ← / →: Step 5 seconds
  • J / K / L: Scrub backward / Pause / Forward

Quick Editing & Gestures

  • Click Text on Video: Open Quick Edit Bubble
  • Click Strip Above Timeline: Inspect active subtitle
  • Click Timeline Pill: Seek directly to cue
  • Ctrl + Z / Ctrl + Y: Undo / Redo changes
  • S: Split active cue at current playhead
  • Esc: Close active bubble / popover / modal

Timeline & Tracks

  • Ctrl + Mouse Wheel: Zoom timeline in / out
  • Click & Drag Playhead: Smooth video scrubbing
  • Subtitles Track Header: Add new Kurdish cue at playhead
  • Video Track Header: Choose or swap video file
  • Audio / Watermark Track Header: Add background audio or watermark
